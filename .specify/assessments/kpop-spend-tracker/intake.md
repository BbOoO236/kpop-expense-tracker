## Idea (as captured)

> 我想要做一个小程序，目标人群是kpop追星族，1.用于用户记录追星所有花销，每一笔花销可以设置可见范围，分为仅自己可见、好友可见、公开可见。2.可以加好友，可以自己选定几个朋友并发起消费审批，征询花销意见。3.专门有一个线下追星模块，可以填写目的地和出发地，记录线下演唱会的每一笔花销以及总花销，可以给想去线下的人花销范围和建议。4.花销可以选择类别，每笔花销是给哪一个团体、是属于哪方面的花销，在进行花销汇总时可以快速知道给哪个团体哪个方面花费了多少，便于统计。

## Restated

Build a mini-program for K-pop fans to record all their fandom-related spending. Each expense entry has a configurable visibility (self-only / friends / public); users can add friends and initiate a "purchase approval" with selected friends to seek opinions on a purchase; and a dedicated offline/concert module records per-expense and total costs for a trip (origin, destination) and surfaces a cost range and advice for others planning to attend. Each expense is also tagged with an idol group and a spending category, so totals can be broken down by group and by category.
